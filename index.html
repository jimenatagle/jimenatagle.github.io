Explainable machine learning

Reconstructing electricity prices with explainable ML

🏆 Neural Wave 2026 · Overall & AET category winner

Reconstructed quarter-hour electricity prices from hourly data using interpolation and interpretable ML corrections, reducing mean absolute error by 54.1% against a flat hourly baseline.

Applied AIBusiness ImpactMachine LearningTime SeriesEnergy MarketsExplainable AI

Goal

Reconstruct 96 quarter-hour electricity prices from 24 hourly prices across four European market zones.

Challenges

  • Preserving hourly averages while learning useful price corrections.
  • Working with limited available features.
  • Managing a high risk of overfitting with limited predictive information.

Approach

  • Combined a smooth PCHIP interpolation baseline with three LightGBM models for intra-hour slope, curvature and alternating price movements.
  • Constrained learned corrections to sum to zero within each hour, preserving the baseline’s hourly mean.
  • Engineered features from hourly price curves, differences between market zones, calendar information and historical prices.
  • Built a Streamlit dashboard to explore reconstructed curves, model contributions and hours at higher risk of reconstruction error.

Results

  • Overall 1st Prize and AET Project Category Winner, Neural Wave Hackathon 2026, Lugano.
  • 54.1% lower mean absolute error than the flat hourly baseline on September evaluation dates.
  • Flagged high-risk hours with 2.8–3.9× higher reconstruction error than low-risk hours in development evaluation.

AI Systems Engineering

Improving a Board Game Recommender System with Compound AI

Improved recommendation accuracy from 68% to 92% by combining a low-cost language model with Python checks, structured outputs and targeted error analysis.

Applied AICompound AILLM EvaluationPythonNLP

Goal

Select a board game that satisfies a natural-language request, or decline when none of the five candidates meets every requirement.

Challenges

  • Distinguishing strict requirements from preferences in natural language.
  • Handling ambiguous descriptions and labels that humans can interpret differently.
  • Improving accuracy while keeping model cost and latency low.

Approach

  • Built a compound system that separates language interpretation, constraint checking and final selection.
  • Converted natural-language requests into structured, validated requirements.
  • Used Python to check explicit constraints such as player count, duration and age.
  • Rejected incompatible candidates before making further model calls, and stopped once a valid recommendation was found.
  • Used the model to interpret descriptive evidence, with supporting quotes verified in Python.
  • Grouped failures by cause and evaluated individual steps to identify where errors originated and target improvements.

Results

MetricSingle-call baselineFinal compound system
Accuracy68%92%
Model cost per request~$0.0004~$0.00051
Optimization

Evolutionary computation for optimizing distribution systems

A genetic algorithm that optimizes distribution layouts to reduce delivery costs under real-world constraints.

Applied AIOptimizationGenetic AlgorithmsBusiness Impact

Goal

Minimize delivery cost by relocating assets to distribution points.

Challenges

  • A large permutation search space makes exact methods impractical on realistic instances
  • Individuals have permutable characteristics
  • Relocations must remain realistically constrained

Approach

  • Stochastic approximation with a genetic algorithm
  • Initialization, crossover and mutation operators adapted to the problem constraints

Results

  • Three times lower delivery cost than traditional methods while respecting relocation constraints
  • A robust, effective and flexible method for resource allocation

Computer Vision3D ReconstructionImage Processing

Goal

Understand and explain the Structure from Motion pipeline from SfM Revisited, then connect the theory to an observed 3D reconstruction workflow.

Technical Focus

  • Feature detection and matching
  • Camera pose estimation, triangulation and bundle adjustment
  • Geometry-based 3D reconstruction

Benefits

  • Efficient reconstruction for modest image collections
  • No model training or generative AI
  • Geometry-based, reproducible results grounded in observed image features

Applied Case

Reconstruction of Statua La Desolazione in Parco Ciani, used to observe how theoretical stages appear in practical reconstruction outputs.

Outcome

Delivered a class presentation that made the SfM pipeline visually understandable, from image features to the final reconstructed structure.

Quality analytics

Predicting & explaining textile rejects

Most production records have no rejected fabric, but occasional failures can cause significant losses. This project identifies high-risk production conditions and explains the factors associated with reject occurrence and severity.

Machine LearningStatistical ModelingQuality AnalyticsBusiness Impact

Goal

Identify which adjustable production settings and fixed product characteristics are associated with rejects, and determine whether reject occurrence and severity have different causes.

Challenges

  • Highly imbalanced target with rare but severe losses (zero-inflated, heavy-tailed)
  • Different mechanisms govern reject occurrence and reject severity
  • Strong requirement for interpretability and decision support

Approach

Compared statistical models for different data distributions and used XGBoost to test nonlinear relationships. The selected two-stage model estimates reject probability with Logistic Regression and severity with Gamma Regression.

Key Findings

  • A second fabric loop was associated with ~2.2× higher reject odds
  • Additional yarn loops were associated with ~1.7× higher reject odds
  • Fabric construction and composition had a stronger effect on loss severity
  • The highest-risk group had ~2.6× the average loss
  • Catastrophic losses are largely driven by latent factors not captured in standard process data

Business Value

  • Prioritizes production records for inspection and monitoring
  • Identifies settings and product characteristics associated with quality risk
  • Separates prevention factors (affect whether a reject occurs) from loss-containment factors (affect how severe the loss becomes after a reject occurs)
  • Shows which additional data could help explain extreme losses

Fraud analytics

Temporal & network analytics for fraud detection

Detecting suspicious financial accounts by combining time-series behavior with transaction-network dependencies.

Machine LearningGraph AnalyticsAnomaly DetectionTime Series

Goal

Identify unusual account behavior and relationships that may indicate fraud.

Challenges

  • Separate trends and seasonality from anomalous behavior
  • Model dependencies across many interacting accounts
  • Handle highly imbalanced fraud labels

Approach

  • Decompose daily account activity into trend, seasonality and residuals
  • Estimate a sparse precision matrix from standardized residuals, treating observations as IID
  • Use partial correlations to measure relationships between two accounts while controlling for all others
  • Compare supervised Random Forest classification with unsupervised Isolation Forest anomaly detection
